About me

I'm a backend, systems and machine learning engineer working in AI and computer vision, and a Computer Science student at the Rochester Institute of Technology. I work end to end: designing APIs and data pipelines, training models in PyTorch, and deploying them as reliable, self-hosted services on Linux.

Most of what I build runs on local hardware. I've trained detection models for a public-safety network of 12,000+ cameras, built offline speech pipelines, served ML models through Django, self-hosted services behind Nginx and Cloudflare Tunnel, and run 35B-parameter LLMs on a single consumer GPU. I'm currently working on EEG-to-image decoding with Prof. John Thomas's group for the NeurIPS 2026 EEG/EMG Challenge.

Alongside AI, I work in cybersecurity. I've done OSINT threat analysis for a counter-terrorism department and I'm an active member of RITSEC. I'm looking for Summer 2027 internships where I can build backend, systems or ML software that people rely on.
